100 Missions. 6 Levels.

One Extraordinary Future.

Every great inventor, scientist, and technology leader started with a single idea. The AI & Robotics Hero Journey is a unique adventure where young innovators learn to imagine, create, code, build, invent, and lead.

From creating their first AI character to designing future technologies, children progress through 100 exciting missions and transform from an Explorer into a Champion.
