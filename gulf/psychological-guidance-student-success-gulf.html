Emotional Resilience in Expat Children: Supporting Focus and Motivation

Expat children often navigate new schools, languages and social circles. Academic pressure can stack on top. Psychological guidance in an educational setting means noticing overwhelm, teaching recovery habits and knowing when families should seek specialised support.

Why well-being matters for STEM

Debugging requires patience. Creative projects require risk-taking. Children who fear failure avoid the practice that builds skill. Mentors who normalise struggle protect motivation.
