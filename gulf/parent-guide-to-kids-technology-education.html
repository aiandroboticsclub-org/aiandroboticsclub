From Screen Time to Creation Time: How Parents Can Guide Productive Technology Habits

Screen time is a weak metric. An hour of debugging a game teaches more than an hour of random video scrolling. A parent guide to kids technology education across the Gulf should help adults coach creation: set a small goal, stay available, and refuse to finish the project for the child.

Guiding questions

What does this block do? What failed last time? What will you try next? Questions transfer ownership. Answers typed by parents do not.
