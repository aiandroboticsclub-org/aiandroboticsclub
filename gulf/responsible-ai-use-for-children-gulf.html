Digital Citizenship in the Age of AI: Understanding Privacy, Bias, and Deepfakes

Ethics is not an optional extra chapter after “fun AI.” Responsible AI use and ethics for kids online should sit beside every experiment. Children need language for privacy, bias and synthetic media without panic or cynicism.

Privacy and the digital footprint

What you upload can travel further than intended. Students practise asking permission, minimising personal data, and choosing safer examples for school projects.

Bias is learnable

Systems reflect their training examples. Learners redesign sample sets to improve fairness in classroom demos—practical ethics, not lectures alone.

Deepfakes and healthy doubt

Age-appropriate discussion covers that realistic media can be fabricated. Verification habits matter more than memorising tool names.
