The Physics of Motion: Keyframes, Timing, and Spacing in Digital 2D Animation

Animation is organised change over time. OpenToonz animation classes for kids online across the Gulf can teach that organisation: a key pose, the spaces between poses, and the timing that makes motion feel heavy or light. Children practise sequencing—the same habit that helps coding and storytelling.

What a keyframe is

A keyframe marks an important pose. In-betweens fill the journey. Learners discover that fewer, clearer keys often read better than frantic random frames.

Timing and spacing

Close spacing can slow a motion; wide spacing can speed it. That is physics-flavoured thinking without formal equations—ideal for creative technology pathways.

Software reality
