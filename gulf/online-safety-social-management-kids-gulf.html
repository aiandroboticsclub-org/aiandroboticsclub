Navigating the Digital World Safely: Protecting Children and Teens Online

Children meet peers, games and AI tools through screens. Online safety education for Gulf families should be practical: what never to share, how to treat others, and when to walk away. Social skill management sits beside technical rules—tone, empathy and self-control matter as much as password hygiene.

Core privacy rules

Children should never share private passwords, home addresses or sensitive personal information with online strangers or into public AI tools. Mentors and parents can rehearse refusal scripts so the habit is automatic under pressure.

Social skills in digital spaces

Kindness, listening and disagreement without insults transfer from playground to chat. Age-appropriate discussions of cyberbullying and reporting pathways belong with trusted adults.
