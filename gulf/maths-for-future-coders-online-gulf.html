Coordinate Geometry in Code: Mapping Characters and Objects on a Cartesian Plane

A Cartesian plane is a grid with horizontal and vertical axes. Characters in many games live on that grid even when the art hides the numbers. Maths for future coders online in the UAE can make those numbers visible: increasing x moves right; increasing y moves up.

From classroom graph to code

Translating a point into variables x and y bridges subjects. A small program that prints a new position after each move reinforces addition and subtraction with purpose.

Distance and decisions

Comparing positions supports simple rules: if the character is near the edge, stop. The educational win is connecting number sense to behaviour.
