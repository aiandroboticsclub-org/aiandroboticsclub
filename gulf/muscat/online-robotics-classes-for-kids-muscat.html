What learning looks like week to week

A productive week usually includes one guided concept, one short practice challenge and one moment where the child explains the idea in their own words. That explanation is more valuable than a polished demo produced by an adult. Mentors model the same restraint online: questions first, answers second, ownership always with the learner.

Debugging as a habit, not a humiliation

When code or circuits fail, the useful question is not "who is to blame" but "what changed since the last working state?" Children learn to change one variable at a time, write down observations and test again. Parents can reinforce the habit by celebrating careful checks as highly as flashy results.
