The Value of Live Mentorship: Overcoming Learning Hurdles in Real-Time

Self-paced videos are patient; they cannot see your screen. Live mentorship can. Saturday live support coding club sessions give learners a weekly window to ask, show and unblock—turning a lonely bug into a shared puzzle.
