Training Tracks for Schools

The same signature pathways we offer families—adapted for group or school schedules when confirmed.

Robotics for Kids (Level 1)

Ages 12–16 · 4–8 weeks · On-Campus & Online

Basics of robotics, safe materials, miniature models, sensors, motors and creative engineering.

Arduino Robotics (Level 2)

Ages 12–16 · Advanced makers

Arduino IDE, sensors, motors, line-following and obstacle-avoiding projects under supervised practice.

Scratch Coding & Games

Ages 10–16 · MIT Scratch

Logic, game design, story animations and drag-and-drop programming with completion certificates.

AI Robotics for Kids

Ages 12–16 · 4–6 weeks

Child-friendly AI basics, simple ML ideas, safety & ethics, and small creative AI experiments.

Animation & Digital Art

Ages 10–16 · OpenToonz + Canva

2D animation, storyboarding, character work and digital design for creative STEAM clubs.

Model Building & Engineering

Ages 12–16 · Physical Lahore

Miniatures, robot mascots and craft-based robotics with safe materials (EVA foam, cardboard, etc.).
