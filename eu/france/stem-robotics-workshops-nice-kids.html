Project-Based Engineering in Nice: Transforming Screen Time into Creative Hardware Logic

Workshops succeed when every session produces a visible attempt—not necessarily a perfect robot. STEM robotics workshops for kids in Nice emphasise the engineering loop: define a goal, try a structure or behaviour, test, revise. Screen time becomes active when the child is deciding.

Online delivery is the primary model. Custom group requests can be discussed; this page does not invent a Nice campus schedule. Simulators and carefully chosen starter activities keep early weeks accessible. When physical materials appear, adult supervision is required for tools, batteries and small parts.

Constraints make creativity sharper. Limited materials and limited time force prioritisation. Students learn to choose the highest-impact fix first. Documentation can be a photo plus two sentences—the habit of recording beats the myth of overnight genius builds.

Parents in coastal France often balance school, languages and extracurriculars. Short, focused challenges fit better than marathon sessions. Ask what failed and what they would try next. Safety: supervise tools and power sources; prefer low-voltage materials.
