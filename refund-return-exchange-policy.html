1. Class Fee Refunds (In-Person & Online)

Refunds are allowed ONLY in the following cases:

Refunds cannot be issued for:

  • One-day workshops, competitions or event tickets
  • DIY kits / robotics kits once opened or used

Return & Exchange Policy

AI and Robotics Club – Cash on Delivery Store

1. Eligibility for Return or Exchange

You can request a return or exchange if:

  • The product arrives damaged, broken or defective
  • The wrong item was delivered
  • Packaging is torn or opened before delivery

You must contact us within 24 hours of receiving the parcel.

2. Conditions

To be eligible for a return/exchange:

  • The product must be unused.
  • All accessories, manuals and packaging must be intact.
  • You must provide photos or videos as proof.

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Used robotics kits
  • Opened boxes
  • Tools damaged after use
  • Digital products
  • Competition tickets or workshop registrations
  • Custom-made items

4. Replacement Process

If the claim is valid:

  • We will send a replacement unit, or
  • Provide store credit for a future purchase

Return shipping cost will be covered by AI and Robotics Club only for damaged or wrong items.

5. Return for Change of Mind

Not allowed due to Cash on Delivery and limited stock nature.
