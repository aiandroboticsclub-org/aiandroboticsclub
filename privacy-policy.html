Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At AI & Robotics Club, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us while placing orders or browsing our store.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or interact with our site, we may collect:

  • Contact details provided during WhatsApp checkout (e.g., name, phone number, delivery address).
  • Local browser data such as items saved in your local shopping cart.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the provided information solely to processing and delivering your orders, responding to your inquiries, and improving your store experience.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your shopping cart details are stored locally on your device via browser local storage and are not transmitted to external tracking servers. Communication regarding orders occurs directly through WhatsApp.

4. Third-Party Services

Order details are transmitted via WhatsApp when you initiate checkout. Please review WhatsApp's Privacy Policy regarding their end-to-end encryption and data practices.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our official WhatsApp contact or email.
