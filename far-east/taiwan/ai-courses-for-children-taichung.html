1. Beyond Averages

Means alone hide spread. Error bars on bars and confidence bands on lines show how much values vary around a central estimate.

2. Error Bars on Category Means

import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

df = pd.read_csv("ecommerce_orders.csv")
stats = df.groupby("Product Category")["Revenue"].agg(["mean", "std"])

fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(8, 5))
ax.bar(
    stats.index,
    stats["mean"],
    yerr=stats["std"],
    capsize=5,
    color="skyblue",
    edgecolor="black"
)
ax.set_title("Mean Revenue by Product Category with Error Bars (Std Dev)")
ax.set_xlabel("Product Category")
ax.set_ylabel("Mean Revenue ($)")
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

yerr draws vertical whiskers; capsize sets the width of the end caps.

3. Confidence Band on Monthly Revenue

df_sample = df.head(500).copy()
df_sample["Order Date"] = pd.to_datetime(df_sample["Order Date"])
df_sample = df_sample.set_index("Order Date")
monthly = df_sample["Revenue"].resample("ME").agg(["mean", "std"])

fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(10, 6))
ax.plot(monthly.index, monthly["mean"], color="blue", label="Mean Revenue")
ax.fill_between(
    monthly.index,
    monthly["mean"] - monthly["std"],
    monthly["mean"] + monthly["std"],
    color="blue",
    alpha=0.3,
    label="±1 Std Dev Confidence Band"
)
ax.tick_params(axis="x", labelrotation=45)
ax.set_title("Monthly Mean Revenue with Confidence Band (±1 Std Dev)")
ax.legend()
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

fill_between shades mean±std; alpha=0.3 keeps the line readable.

4. Parameter Cheat Sheet

ParameterRole
yerrError whiskers on bars
capsizeHorizontal cap width
fill_betweenShaded band between bounds
alphaBand transparency
labelrotationAngle x labels (e.g. 45°)

Lesson Q&A

The mean alone hides spread. With yerr=std, viewers see both the average and how far individual observations typically sit above or below it (e.g. Beauty mean ~1000 with range roughly 250–2000).

It sets the width of the small horizontal caps at the ends of each error whisker so the upper and lower limits are easier to read.

It fills between a lower series (mean−std) and upper series (mean+std) along a shared x index. alpha=0.3 makes the band translucent so the central line and grid stay visible.
